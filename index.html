HomoGrown

A small-scale, sustainable floral farm growing beautiful, heartfelt blooms in South Jersey.

Rooted in Passion

Hi, we’re Richie and Kevin. What started as a labor of love in our Stratford backyard has blossomed into HomoGrown. We are a small-scale farm dedicated to cultivating specialty cut flowers, nurturing native plants, and promoting a culture of re-wilding.

We believe deeply in sustainable growing practices. From amending our soil naturally in our greenhouse to carefully cultivating our beloved dahlias and native swamp milkweed, every seed we sow is grown with intention and care.

We recently had our very first sale at the Barrington Harvest Festival, and we were absolutely blown away by the community's support. Thank you to everyone who stopped by! We are currently working hard behind the scenes to expand our garden and prepare for our next growing season.
